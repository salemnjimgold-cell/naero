# Naero Color Systems — 7 Proposals

Research basis: Apple, Airbnb, Stripe, Linear, Arc, Notion, Revolut, Spotify, Google, Nothing.
Each palette tested against: warmth, trust, premium feel, brand recognizability, and suitability for immigrants + AI.

---

**Inspired by:** Airbnb's warmth, Aesop's amber, Calm's twilight.
**Philosophy:** Warm darkness lit by candlelight. Safety through warmth.

| Token | Hex |
|---|---|

| Surface | #F0EBE3 |
| Text Primary | #1A1611 |

**Emotion:** Safety, warmth, home. Like sitting by a fireplace. The color of being welcomed.

**Brand:** Instantly recognizable as "the warm app." No other major app owns amber-gold as primary.

## System 7: "Obsidian"

**Inspired by:** Nothing's monochrome, Porsche's precision, Apple's Pro products.
**Philosophy:** Pure contrast. No color is a color. Precision through absence.

**Brand:** Nothing proves monochrome creates identity. Risk: hard to feel warm.
