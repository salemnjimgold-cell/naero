# Emergency & Safety Guide for Hungary

## Emergency Numbers

| Service | Number | Description |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| General Emergency | 112 | EU standard, English-speaking operators available |
| Ambulance | 104 | Medical emergencies |
| Fire Department | 105 | Fire and rescue |
| Police | 107 | Reporting crimes |

## Lost or Stolen Documents

### Lost Passport
1. Report to local police station (get a police report)
2. Contact your embassy or consulate
3. Apply for emergency travel document
4. Your embassy can provide replacement procedures

### Lost Residence Permit
1. Report to immigration office (Bevándorlási Hivatal)
2. File a police report
3. Apply for replacement card
4. Processing time: 30-60 days

## Mental Health Support
- **24/7 Crisis Helpline**: 116-123 (free)
- **Blue Line (Child Helpline)**: 116-111
- **Hungarian Mental Health Association**: +36 1 302 1040
- Private English-speaking therapists available in Budapest

## Crime Prevention
- Budapest is generally safe, but watch for pickpocketing in tourist areas
- Keep valuables in hotel safe
- Use licensed taxis (BKK, Főtaxi) or ride-sharing apps
- Avoid poorly lit areas at night
- Drink spiking can occur in bars - watch your drink

## Natural Disasters
- Hungary has no earthquakes or hurricanes
- Flooding can occur along the Danube and Tisza rivers
- Heavy snowfall can disrupt transport in winter
- Heat waves common in summer (35-40°C)
