# Housing Guide for Hungary

## Renting an Apartment

### Finding Apartments
- Popular portals: ingatlan.com, alberlet.hu, facebook groups
- Real estate agents charge 1-2 months rent as commission
- Most listings in Hungarian - use Google Translate or ask a Hungarian speaker

### Rental Process
1. Find an apartment and schedule a viewing
2. Agree on terms with the landlord or agent
3. Sign a rental contract (bérleti szerződés)
4. Pay the deposit (kaució) - typically 2-3 months rent
5. Pay the first month's rent
6. Register your address at the district office

### Rental Contract Essentials
- Must include: parties' details, property address, rent amount, duration, notice period
- Should specify: utility payment arrangement, deposit terms, maintenance responsibilities
- Recommended to have a bilingual contract
- Contract must be registered with the tax office (NAV) by the landlord

### Tenant Rights
- Deposit must be returned within 30 days of move-out (minus damages)
- 30-day notice period typically required
- Landlord must give 24-hour notice for inspections
- Rent increases are limited to inflation rate unless otherwise specified
- You have the right to a habitable property

### Common Costs
- Rent: 1-bedroom in Budapest center: 250,000-400,000 HUF
- Utilities (electricity, gas, water, internet): 30,000-60,000 HUF/month
- Condominium fee (közös költség): 5,000-20,000 HUF/month
- Agency fee: 1-2 months rent (one-time)
- Deposit: 2-3 months rent

## Buying Property

### Process for Foreigners
- EU/EEA citizens: can buy freely
- Non-EU citizens: need official permission from the local government office
- Permission typically takes 30-60 days
- Notary fees: 1-2% of property value
- Property transfer tax: 4% (up to 1 billion HUF), then 9%

## Useful Hungarian Phrases
- "Kiadó lakás" - Apartment for rent
- "Eladó lakás" - Apartment for sale
- "Mennyi a bérleti díj?" - How much is the rent?
- "Szeretném megnézni a lakást" - I'd like to see the apartment
