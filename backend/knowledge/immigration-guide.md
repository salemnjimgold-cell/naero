# Immigration Guide for Hungary

## Visa Types

### Short-Stay Visa (Schengen C Visa)
- Valid for stays up to 90 days within a 180-day period
- Required for tourists, business visitors, and family visits
- Apply at the Hungarian embassy or consulate in your home country
- Processing time: 15-30 calendar days

### National Visa (D Visa)
- For stays longer than 90 days
- Required for work, study, or family reunification
- Apply before entering Hungary
- Must register with immigration authorities within 30 days of arrival

## Residency Permits

### EU/EEA Citizens
- Free to enter and stay for up to 90 days
- Must register with local immigration office for stays over 90 days
- Need to prove employment, self-sufficiency, or study
- Registration card valid for 5 years

### Non-EU Citizens
- Must obtain a residence permit before or shortly after arrival
- Types: employment, student, family reunification, guest investor
- Processing time: 30-90 days
- Cards typically valid for 1-2 years, renewable

### Work Permit
- Required for non-EU citizens
- Employer must obtain a work permit from the immigration office
- Combined residence and work permit available
- Highly skilled workers may qualify for the White Card or EU Blue Card

## Permanent Residence

### Eligibility
- Continuous legal residence for 3-5 years (varies by permit type)
- Stable income and accommodation
- Health insurance
- Basic knowledge of Hungarian language (A1 level for some categories)
- No criminal record

### Application Process
- Submit application at the regional immigration office
- Required documents: passport, residence permits, proof of income, accommodation, health insurance
- Decision within 3-6 months
- Permanent residence permit valid for 5 years, renewable

## Citizenship

### Eligibility
- 8 years of continuous residence
- Pass a Hungarian language and culture exam (B1 level)
- Stable income, accommodation
- No criminal record
- Renounce previous citizenship (Hungary typically does not allow dual citizenship)

### Naturalization Process
- Language exam: basic conversation and writing
- Citizenship exam: Hungarian history, culture, and constitution
- Interview with immigration authorities
- Decision by the President of Hungary
