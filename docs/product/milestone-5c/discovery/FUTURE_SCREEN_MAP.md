# Future Screen Map

## Primary navigation

1. **Home** — orientation, urgent route, next step, Ask Naero and resume.
2. **Discover** — search, nearby, city guides, verified services and practical categories.
3. **Plan** — user-started actions, requirements, saved guides and progress. Initially ship as Saved until the task model is real.
4. **My Naero** — city, language, saved/history, privacy, notifications and account.

## Secondary screens

- Universal search and results
- Place/service detail
- Guide/procedure detail
- Category and collection results
- Job/housing detail only after real-data readiness
- Safety and emergency information
- Notification center
- Saved collection
- Plan/task detail and source update history
- Community topic/question/detail

## Modal and contextual experiences

- Language picker
- Location rationale and mode switcher
- Manual city search
- Source/provenance sheet
- Save-to-collection
- Report outdated/unsafe information
- Share/export
- Confirmation for privacy-sensitive/destructive actions
- Authentication upgrade when a guest requests sync/account-only behavior

## AI surfaces

- Persistent Ask Naero action
- Full conversation
- Explain selected content
- Compare selected results
- Turn reviewed content into steps
- Contextual clarification sheet
- Source/assumption/uncertainty panel
- Translation view

## Account and settings

- Account and security
- Language and display
- Current city and location privacy
- AI data/preferences and conversation controls
- Notifications
- Saved/history retention
- Accessibility and reduced motion
- Blocked/reported users if Community launches
- About, sources policy, terms and privacy

## Future-only modules

- Reviewed residency/administrative plan engine
- Real jobs and housing ecosystems
- Verified contributor program
- Events and groups
- Offline essential packs
- Change alerts
- Background location
- Privacy-preserving nearby presence

Future-only destinations must remain absent or explicitly preview-labeled until their data, moderation, safety and operational ownership exist.
