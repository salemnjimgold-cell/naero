# Naero Product Thesis

## Mission

Naero helps a person turn an unfamiliar place into a life they can navigate with confidence. It combines structured, source-aware guidance with local context and optional conversational help. The user remains the decision-maker; Naero reduces uncertainty, reveals the next useful action, and explains why it matters.

## Product promise

> **When I open Naero, I know that I can understand where I am, identify what matters next, and act without facing the new place alone.**

The primary promise is orientation-to-action: one trustworthy, contextually relevant next move rather than a wall of features or an unbounded answer.

Secondary promises:

- I can get useful value as a guest and without location access.
- I can distinguish official facts, verified records, community knowledge, external listings, and AI interpretation.
- I can explore at my own pace without being framed as helpless.
- My language, privacy choices, saved knowledge, and current city remain under my control.
- Urgent needs take a shorter path than exploratory needs.

Naero must never become a generic feed, an undifferentiated directory, an authority-impersonating AI, a bureaucratic form maze, or a product that manufactures personalization from invented data.

## Emotional contract

The user may arrive curious, capable, rushed, lonely, overloaded, or afraid of an expensive mistake—often several at once. Naero should move them from **uncertain → oriented → capable → connected**, without dramatizing migration or treating the user as vulnerable by default.

The desired character is calm competence with human warmth: “I can handle this, and reliable help is close.” Dignity means permission requests have alternatives, guidance gives reasons, uncertainty is visible, and the user can always choose another route.

## Core intent model

1. **Help me now** — emergency, safety, healthcare, urgent practical support.
2. **Help me understand** — explain a process, term, rule, place, or local norm.
3. **Help me find** — locate a relevant place or verified service nearby or in a chosen city.
4. **Help me do** — turn a life task into steps, requirements, sources, and an action.
5. **Help me settle** — maintain a small personal plan, saved resources, and next steps.
6. **Help me belong** — obtain bounded, practical community knowledge or connection.
7. **Help me explore** — learn the city beyond urgent newcomer needs.

These are verbs and outcomes, not database categories. Housing, jobs, residency, healthcare, transportation, language, and safety appear beneath the intent that brought the user in.

## Retention after month one

Naero earns continued installation by remembering useful context with consent, maintaining trusted saved items and action plans, surfacing changes that matter, supporting recurring local discovery, and becoming the quickest route from “I am unsure” to “I know my next move.” It must graduate with the user from arrival support to everyday local confidence.

## Competitive expectations

- From Maps: fast search, location clarity, directions, honest distance and hours.
- From government apps: source authority, procedural precision, version dates.
- From Airbnb/Uber: transparent status, predictable actions, recovery from failure.
- From Duolingo: visible progress, but without gamifying serious life tasks.
- From community products: human experience and reciprocity, without an addictive feed.
- From AI assistants: natural language and synthesis, with stronger boundaries and provenance.
- From relocation platforms: task relevance, but available continuously rather than as a one-time checklist.

Naero should use familiar controls for search, saving, back navigation, permissions, and source links. Its distinctive layer is the relationship between city context, trustworthy next steps, and restrained intelligence.

## The soul of Naero

Naero’s soul is **a compass made practical**: it notices context, names the next decision, shows the evidence, and steps aside while the user acts. The signature is not an orb or palette alone. It is the repeated interaction pattern: **Orient → Explain → Act → Remember**.
