# Direction A — Contextual Compass

**Philosophy:** Naero quietly turns a user’s real context into one trustworthy next move, then steps aside.

- **Emotional character:** calm competence, humane warmth, agency.
- **Home:** city/mode header; urgent utility; one evidence-backed “Next useful step”; Ask Naero; a small nearby/saved/changed section chosen from real data.
- **Navigation:** Home / Discover / Plan / My Naero; contextual AI action.
- **AI role:** interpreter across the product, with a full conversation only when ambiguity benefits from it.
- **Discover:** practical city lens combining verified services, nearby places and reviewed guides, organized by intent.
- **Community:** bounded answers and local notes attached to topics/places; no generic feed.
- **Personal area:** My Naero holds plans, saved items, current city, language, privacy, history and account controls.
- **Visual philosophy:** a new hybrid—Deep Sea’s calm structural navy, warmer text/surfaces, emerald reserved for guidance, amber for saved/progress. Light mode should eventually be first-class.
- **Typography/surfaces:** globally capable humanist sans; reading-led hierarchy; fewer, differentiated surfaces; cards only for discrete objects/actions.
- **Imagery/icons:** authentic city/document detail where useful; culturally neutral line icons; compass used sparingly as a guidance state.
- **Spacing/elevation:** generous content rhythm, low elevation, borders and tonal layers over shadows.
- **Motion:** state transitions and orientation only; compass moves only while genuinely working; reduced-motion parity.
- **AI identity:** a small directional marker plus explicit source/uncertainty language—not a face or omnipresent orb.

## Strengths

Strongest match to mission; differentiates from Maps and chatbots; works from sparse current data; scales from arrival to settled life; preserves user dignity.

## Weaknesses and complexity

High product/content complexity. A trustworthy “next step” requires a real task model and governance; until then Home must use honest setup or saved/recent actions. Implementation complexity: **high, staged**.

## Reuse and scalability

High reuse of auth, location, nearby, AI, notification, saved-state, theme primitives and current three-tab philosophy. Home content, data provenance UI, personal area and eventual Plan require meaningful redesign. Long-term scalability: **excellent**.
