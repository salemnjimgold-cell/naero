# Information Architecture Options

## Option 1 — Companion Core (recommended)

**Home / Discover / Plan / My Naero**, with a persistent contextual “Ask Naero” action.

- Home orients and offers one evidence-backed next action.
- Discover covers nearby and city knowledge.
- Plan contains user-created or accepted tasks, not fabricated progress.
- My Naero holds saved items, city/language/privacy, notifications and account.
- AI opens from Home, search, detail pages, plan steps and a persistent action; it is not a competing content silo.

Why: it balances first-month needs with long-term utility and gives the companion promise a durable action model. Risk: Plan must remain honest and is not ready until persistence/content governance exists.

## Option 2 — Immediate Utility

**Today / Find / Ask / Me**.

Why: extremely legible, makes AI first-class, and can reuse current screens quickly. “Today” can work with sparse personalization. Weakness: a permanent Ask tab reinforces chatbot-wrapper perception, while tasks and community become secondary.

## Option 3 — Place and People

**Home / City / Community / My Naero**, with contextual AI.

Why: close to current Home/World/People and maximizes reuse. It offers a clear local identity. Weakness: Community is not production-real, and practical life tasks lack an obvious home.

## IA rules

- Primary navigation represents repeated user jobs, not every content type.
- Safety must be reachable from Home and search in two interactions or fewer, never buried as a tab requirement.
- Jobs, housing, healthcare, administration and transportation are domains inside Discover/Plan, not permanent tabs.
- Search spans places, practical guides, verified services and saved items while retaining source type.
- A user can switch city or use location-off mode without entering Settings.
- Community never appears on Home as personalized people until the data, consent and moderation model is real.
- AI deep-links into structured results; normal UI handles comparison, filters, status, permissions, saving and destructive actions.

## Recommended launch sequencing

Until Plan is backed by real data, ship the recommended architecture progressively as **Home / Discover / Saved / My Naero**, then graduate Saved to Plan when durable tasks are available. Do not display invented progress to preserve the future label.
