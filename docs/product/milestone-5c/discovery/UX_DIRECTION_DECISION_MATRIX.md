## Interpretation

Direction B leads on immediate trust, discovery and feasibility because Naero already has a credible nearby/verified-service foundation. Its weakness is strategic: without a journey layer it converges on Maps plus guides.

Direction C is emotionally strongest but scores poorly on trust and feasibility because community quality cannot be designed into existence; moderation, identity, supply and privacy must precede prominence.

Direction A wins because it makes structured utility, restrained AI and emotional continuity reinforce each other. Its lower feasibility score is a warning to stage the Plan/next-step promise and never substitute mock personalization.
