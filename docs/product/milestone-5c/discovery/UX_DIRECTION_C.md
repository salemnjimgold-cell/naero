# Direction C — Local Weave

**Philosophy:** Help newcomers build confidence through small, trustworthy circles of local knowledge and mutual help.

- **Emotional character:** belonging, reciprocity, optimism, human presence.
- **Home:** city circle; current questions; practical requests; relevant event/group; direct urgent utility.
- **Navigation:** Home / Circles / Discover / Me; AI is a facilitator inside questions and summaries.
- **AI role:** translates, clusters duplicate questions, summarizes discussions with attribution, and routes unanswered needs—never impersonates a person.
- **Discover:** community-enriched places and services, emphasizing “why this helps” over exhaustive coverage.
- **Community:** topic/city/language circles, practical Q&A, verified contributors and bounded events; no follower counts or infinite entertainment feed.
- **Personal area:** contributions, saved answers/places, circles, privacy, blocks/reports and account.
- **Visual philosophy:** warmer Guided Compass base with cream, earth-neutral surfaces, emerald actions and restrained documentary photography.
- **Typography/surfaces:** conversational reading hierarchy; message/answer structures rather than generic cards.
- **Imagery/icons:** authentic people and local texture only with consent; no stock “vulnerable newcomer” imagery.
- **Spacing/elevation:** generous and tactile, with clear authorship boundaries.
- **Motion:** gentle thread expansion and contribution feedback; no engagement celebration.
- **AI identity:** facilitator label and translation/summarization badge.

## Strengths

Highest warmth and belonging; distinctive if trust and moderation work; community knowledge can reveal needs formal data misses.

## Weaknesses and complexity

Cold-start, abuse, misinformation, privacy and moderation risks are severe. Current Community is mock-backed, so the direction requires substantial infrastructure before it can honestly lead. Implementation complexity: **very high**.

## Reuse and scalability

Moderate reuse of screen shells, auth, AI and location; low reuse of current community backend. Long-term scalability: **potentially high but operationally expensive and network-effect dependent**.
