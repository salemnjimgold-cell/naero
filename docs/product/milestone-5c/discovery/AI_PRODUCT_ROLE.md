# AI Product Role

## Role

AI is Naero’s **interpreter and pathfinder**, not its database, authority, personality mascot, or default interface. It translates ambiguity into a structured next move and hands the user back to reliable UI.

## What deserves conversation

- Ambiguous intent: “I just arrived; what should I do first?”
- Explanation in plain language or another language.
- Comparing options with personal constraints.
- Turning a reviewed guide into a checklist.
- Asking follow-up questions when the correct path depends on jurisdiction or status.
- Summarizing several sourced records without hiding their differences.

## What remains normal UI

Search and filters, maps/lists, source and freshness labels, forms, permission controls, settings, saved items, task status, emergency calling, account/security, destructive confirmation, and any action requiring exact review before submission.

## Entry points

- Persistent “Ask Naero” action, subordinate to navigation.
- Home prompt based on real context or a neutral starter when context is absent.
- “Explain this” on trusted content.
- “Help me choose” on comparable results.
- “Turn into steps” on eligible guides.
- “What should I bring?” on place/procedure details.
- Conversational search when keywords fail.

## Proactivity boundaries

AI may suggest help after explicit friction, a new city selection, an incomplete user-created plan, or a relevant source update. It stays silent during emergencies, permissions, authentication, payments, destructive actions, form submission and when confidence/context is insufficient. No behavioral urgency, guilt, or engagement bait.

## Trust behavior

Every consequential answer must separate:

1. **Known facts** with source, jurisdiction and review/update date.
2. **Interpretation** explicitly labeled as AI-generated.
3. **User-specific assumptions** shown and editable.
4. **Unknowns** stated plainly.
5. **Next verification step**, preferably an official source or qualified professional.

AI must say “I could not verify this” rather than completing an answer from plausibility. Legal, residency, healthcare and safety guidance uses cautious language, never guarantees outcomes, never silently mixes jurisdictions, and displays an official verification action near the claim—not only in a disclaimer footer.

## Relationship model

`User intent → AI clarifies → trusted sources/results → structured action → user decides → Naero remembers with consent.`

Success is not conversation length. It is the percentage of sessions where the user reaches a verified result, saves a useful resource, starts/completes a legitimate action, or confidently stops because uncertainty was made visible.
