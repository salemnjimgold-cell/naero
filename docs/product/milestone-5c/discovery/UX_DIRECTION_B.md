# Direction B — Civic Atlas

**Philosophy:** Make the unfamiliar city legible through an authoritative, searchable atlas of practical life.

- **Emotional character:** clarity, reliability, independence, civic confidence.
- **Home:** universal search; current city; urgent shortcuts; recently viewed/saved; authoritative “Know before you go” updates.
- **Navigation:** Today / Find / Guides / My Naero; Ask embedded in search and guides.
- **AI role:** explainer and translator of selected records, not a standalone relationship.
- **Discover:** the core product—map/list, practical categories, filters, verified services, official guides and provenance.
- **Community:** small “local notes” layer on places/guides, heavily moderated.
- **Personal area:** saved atlas, recent history, offline essentials, city/language/privacy/account.
- **Visual philosophy:** evolved Deep Sea with stronger daylight/light-mode support, crisp cartographic hierarchy and restrained blue/teal source coding.
- **Typography/surfaces:** highly legible utility typography, denser information, clear dividers, fewer atmospheric surfaces.
- **Imagery/icons:** maps, document thumbnails and functional symbols; no mascot emphasis.
- **Spacing/elevation:** compact but accessible; sheets over maps; flat lists and grouped sections.
- **Motion:** map/list transitions, status changes and route continuity only.
- **AI identity:** an “Explain” annotation mark; source-first output.

## Strengths

Most immediately credible, easiest to understand, strongest trust posture, closest to current real nearby/verified-service infrastructure, and technically feasible.

## Weaknesses and complexity

Risks becoming a superior directory or quasi-government portal; emotional companionship and long-term journey value are weaker. Content governance becomes the dominant operational cost. Implementation complexity: **medium-high**.

## Reuse and scalability

Very high reuse of Discover, location, gateway, verified-service and detail foundations; modest theme evolution. Long-term scalability: **strong for content/geography, moderate for relationship differentiation**.
