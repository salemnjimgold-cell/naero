# Trust, Privacy and Safety Principles

## Authority model

| Information type | Indicator | Required metadata |
|---|---|---|
| Official | Government/institution seal-style label, never Naero branding alone | Publisher, jurisdiction, source link, effective/reviewed date |
| Naero verified | Distinct verified mark | Verification method, reviewer role, checked date, expiry |
| External listing | Provider label | Provider, retrieval time, attribution, possible limitations |
| Community | Person/group label | Author context, moderation state, report action, timestamp |
| AI guidance | “AI explanation” label | Sources used, assumptions, uncertainty and verification action |

Color may reinforce but never solely communicate authority. A verified place is not the same as an officially authoritative procedure.

## Content safety

- Consequential content has jurisdiction and freshness boundaries.
- Outdated or unverifiable content is visibly downgraded, not silently retained.
- Community advice cannot inherit verified/official styling through AI summarization.
- Housing/jobs require scam reporting and provenance before promotion.
- Emergency screens favor local official numbers and direct action over generated prose.

## Location principles

- Ask only at the moment a location benefit is visible.
- Explain purpose, precision, storage, sharing and duration before the OS prompt.
- Offer manual city and location-off paths with meaningful utility.
- Show the active location mode near location-dependent results.
- Make change/clear controls accessible outside buried settings.
- Foreground location is not permission for background collection.
- Future nearby-user features are explicit opt-in, coarse by default, time-bounded, blockable/reportable, and never expose precise coordinates or passive movement histories.

## Data and AI privacy

- Minimize profile inputs; explain each personalization benefit.
- Do not send unrelated profile, conversation or precise-location data to AI providers.
- Make conversation history behavior and deletion understandable.
- Separate “save this” from implicit model memory.
- Guest data stays local unless the user knowingly signs in/syncs.
- Sensitive actions require confirmation in structured UI.

## International and accessibility baseline

- Design from bidirectional constraints: logical spacing, mirrored directional affordances, correct reading order and mixed-script testing.
- Permit 200% text scaling without loss of content/action; avoid fixed-height text containers.
- Minimum 4.5:1 normal-text contrast and 3:1 large text/essential graphics.
- Minimum 44×44 pt touch targets with non-color state labels.
- Every control has a meaningful accessible name, role, state and error association.
- Reduced-motion mode removes ambient/looping motion; no required information is motion-only.
- Errors name what happened, what was preserved, and at least one recovery path.
- Plain language, progressive disclosure and consistent icon+label pairs support low digital literacy and stressful use.
