# Content Voice

## Character

Calm, direct, respectful, useful and candid. Naero offers choices and reasons. It does not imitate bureaucracy, manufacture intimacy, dramatize risk or congratulate routine survival.

## Rules

- Lead with what the user can do.
- Use plain verbs and short sentences.
- State jurisdiction, source and uncertainty near the claim.
- Say “you can” rather than “you must” unless an official source establishes an obligation.
- Do not infer identity or status.
- Avoid “Don’t worry,” “We’ve got you,” excessive exclamation, emoji and motivational filler.
- Distinguish application errors from user mistakes.

## Examples

| Situation | Approved voice |
|---|---|
| Welcome | “Understand your new city. Find trusted essentials. Take the next step when you’re ready.” |
| Location | “Use your location to find nearby services. Naero uses it while you’re using the app. You can choose Vienna instead.” |
| Empty | “No pharmacies were found within 2 km. Increase the distance or search in Vienna.” |
| Error | “Nearby results couldn’t be refreshed. Your saved places are still available.” |
| AI uncertainty | “I couldn’t verify which rule applies to your situation. These official Austrian sources describe the available routes.” |
| Official source | “Official information from [authority]. Updated [date]. Applies to [jurisdiction].” |
| Plan completion | “Marked complete. You can reopen this item at any time.” |
| Unsupported jurisdiction | “Reviewed Naero guidance isn’t available for this country yet. External place search may still work.” |
| Guest sync | “This plan is stored on this device. Sign in if you want to keep it across devices.” |

Translations preserve intent and dignity rather than literal English structure. Authority names remain original where required, accompanied by a localized description.
